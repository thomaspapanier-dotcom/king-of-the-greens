# Realm landmarks: prompts for ChatGPT

Each of the six Realms gets a **3D landmark** that stands on its part of the Leaderboards map, like the little buildings on a tourist map. Right now each Realm shows a crowned pin in its color; when a landmark image is ready, it replaces the pin.

**How to send them:** use the same ChatGPT chat as the challenge art, so it keeps the style and the Kings. Send the setup message once, then one Realm at a time. Save each image with the file name shown (for example `realm-ivy.png`) in the KOG Images folder and tell me. I'll drop them onto the map.

---

## Step 1: setup message (send once)

```
New set: REALM LANDMARKS for the King of the Greens leaderboard map. The U.S. is split into six Realms, and each Realm needs one landmark that stands on its part of a 3D map, like the miniature buildings on an illustrated tourist map.

Every landmark follows the same rules:
- A small 3D miniature diorama, seen from a 3/4 isometric angle from the front and slightly above, like a board-game piece.
- It sits on a small chunk of land: a tiny putting green with a flag and cup, plus a little of that Realm's scenery.
- A royal touch on every piece: a crown, a pennant or a banner in the Realm's color.
- The Realm's color shows up clearly (in a flag, a roof or a banner) so the piece matches its part of the map.
- Same painted style as our challenge art, but no Kings in these. They're places, not characters.
- Transparent background (PNG), square, the whole piece in frame with a little space around it, and a soft shadow under the base only.
- No text, no real logos, no real brand names, no real people.
Reply "ready" and I'll send them one at a time.
```
