# KOG Challenges

Every challenge earns a **badge** and a **banner**. Players can wear them together or mix and match.
Almost all of them come from **ranked**, where both players confirm the score. Only a few come from Compass Putt and Lag 21.

Rarity ink: **Common** (silver), **Rare** (blue), **Very Rare** (purple), **Impossible** (red).

The Badge and Banner lines describe the final art and can be handed to an image generator.


## Compass Putt

### The North Remembers · Common
- **How to complete (Compass Putt):** Make a Long putt (in on the first putt) from the North tee in Compass Putt.
- **Badge:** The King of Spades as the King in the North: fur cloak, iron crown, frost in his beard, holding his putter like a longsword.
- **Banner:** A frozen castle wall above a snowy practice green under a grey sky, the suited Kings standing watch in fur cloaks.

### West Coast, Best Coast · Common
- **How to complete (Compass Putt):** Make a Long putt from the West tee in Compass Putt.
- **Badge:** The King of Clubs with a bandana and round sunglasses, palm trees behind him, 90s West Coast style.
- **Banner:** Sunset over a California beach course with palm trees and lowriders in the lot, the suited Kings leaning on their putters.

### East Coast, Beast Coast · Common
- **How to complete (Compass Putt):** Make a Long putt from the East tee in Compass Putt.
- **Badge:** The King of Diamonds in a puffer jacket and tan work boots, the Empire State Building behind him.
- **Banner:** A New York rooftop putting green with a subway sign and the skyline at night, the suited Kings in streetwear.

### The Deep South · Common
- **How to complete (Compass Putt):** Make a Long putt from the South tee in Compass Putt.
- **Badge:** The King of Hearts rising out of flames, smoke curling around his crown like horns.
- **Banner:** The suited Kings rising from a fiery, smoky underworld onto a scorched practice green.

### Never Lost · Rare
- **How to complete (Compass Putt):** Earn all four Compass Putt challenges: North, East, South and West.
- **Badge:** A golden compass rose with a crown at North and the four suits at the four points.
- **Banner:** An old treasure map of a golf course with a dotted putt line and a suited King at each compass point.


## Lag 21

### Blackjack · Common
- **How to complete (Lag 21):** Win a game of Lag 21 against at least one other player.
- **Badge:** A blackjack hand: the King of Spades and an Ace, with a golf ball for a chip.
- **Banner:** A green-felt casino table turned into a putting green, chips stacked by the cup and a neon "21" above.


## Eagles

### The Bird with Big Wings · Rare
- **How to complete (Ranked):** Make an eagle putt (sink an eagle ball on the first putt) in a ranked match.
- **Badge:** A bald eagle in a tiny crown, wings spread, clutching a golf ball.
- **Banner:** The suited Kings standing in a golden wheat field while a huge eagle soars overhead.

### Albatross · Very Rare
- **How to complete (Ranked):** Make 2 eagle putts in the same ranked match. (Two eagles make an albatross, golf’s rarest bird.)
- **Badge:** An albatross gliding with two golf balls in its feet.
- **Banner:** Ocean cliffs at sunset with two great seabirds over a seaside green, the Kings watching from the clifftop.

### King of the Skies · Impossible
- **How to complete (Ranked):** Sink all three eagle balls on the first putt in one ranked match.
- **Badge:** A golden eagle wearing the King’s crown, three golf balls set like jewels beneath it.
- **Banner:** Above the clouds: the four Kings on a green floating in the sky, eagles circling a sunburst.

### Flawless Shawty · Very Rare

- **Banner:** A spotless, perfectly striped green under spotlights, nine balls in the cup and the Kings applauding.

### Dead Center · Impossible
- **How to complete (Ranked):** One-putt all 9 balls in the Medium-putt round (Round 4) of a ranked match.
- **Badge:** A bullseye with a golf ball dead center and a crown on top.
- **Banner:** A green with target rings mowed into it, the suited Kings standing with arms crossed.

### Long Live the King · Impossible
- **How to complete (Ranked):** One-putt all 9 balls in the Long-putt round (Round 6) of a ranked match.
- **Badge:** A golden crown floating above a putter, glowing like a holy relic.
- **Banner:** A royal procession on a cathedral-lit green, crowds cheering as the King walks to the cup.

### Royal Flush · Impossible
- **How to complete (Ranked):** One-putt every ball in a regular round: all three sets, Short, Medium and Long.
- **Badge:** Five cards fanned out, 10 to Ace of spades, with a golf ball for the Ace.

- **How to complete (Ranked):** One-putt all 3 balls of a Long set (both birdie balls and the par ball, from 10–15 ft).
- **Badge:** Three golf balls in a row split by a lightning bolt.
- **Banner:** Three long putt lines curving across a green into one cup, each leaving a glowing trail.

## Clutch

### Wire to Wire · Rare

- **Banner:** A finish ribbon stretched across the green, the King breaking the tape.

### Regicide · Rare
