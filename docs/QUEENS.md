# Queen of the Greens

The plan, in order:

1. **Make the four Queen cards** with ChatGPT (prompts below). Keep going until you love all four.
2. **Make the Queen versions of every badge and banner**: same challenge, same scene, same layout, with the Queens in place of the Kings (template prompt below).
3. **Last: the King or Queen choice** when a player verifies their account (it can't be changed later). That gets built once the Queen art exists.

## The four Queens

Each Queen has her own look and personality, different from each other and from the Kings, but all four fit the same world: vintage engraved playing-card art, golf, crowns and suits. Like the classic deck Queens, each one carries a flower.

| Queen | Who she is | Her look | Her flower |
|---|---|---|---|

### Step 1: setup message (send once)

Attach the four King cards (from the KOG Images folder, or `assets/cards/king-*.webp`) and, if you like, pictures of the four Queens from a classic deck of cards. Then send:

```
New characters for King of the Greens: the four suited QUEENS. They join the four Kings (attached) and must look like they belong in the same deck.

STYLE: Match the attached King cards exactly: vintage engraved playing-card illustration, bold ink linework, fine crosshatching, rich warm colors, the same cream card border, corner indices (a "Q" with the suit symbol in two corners) and a golf scene behind each character. Base each Queen loosely on the classic playing-card Queen of her suit (crown, long hair, carrying a flower) but reimagined as a modern golfer with her own personality, the way the Kings were reimagined.

Each Queen must look clearly different from the other three Queens and from the Kings: different style, personality, hair, outfit and pose. No real-world brand logos; use suit symbols and crowns for logos.

FORMAT: portrait playing card, 1024x1536, full figure, the same framing as the King cards.

I'll send one Queen at a time. Make one image per reply and wait for my feedback. Reply "Ready" if you understand.
```

## Later: Queen versions of the badges and banners

Once the four Queens are final, every challenge gets a Queen badge and banner. Same challenge, same name, same scene and layout, with each King swapped for the Queen of the same suit (King of Spades → Queen of Spades, and so on). Attach the King version and send:

```
Recreate this [badge / banner] exactly: same scene, same composition, same colors, same rim and rarity look, same layout. The only change: replace every King with the Queen of the same suit (King of Spades → Queen of Spades, King of Hearts → Queen of Hearts, King of Diamonds → Queen of Diamonds, King of Clubs → Queen of Clubs), doing the same action in the same pose, in her own style.
```

Save them with `queen-` in front of the usual name: `queen-badge-north.png`, `queen-banner-north.png`. I'll keep a list of which ones are done, like the King art.

## Ideas: King and Queen challenges (not added yet)

Each one has a King version and a Queen version, so players earn the one that matches the ruler they picked.

| Idea | If you're a King | If you're a Queen |
|---|---|---|
| **Royal Rivalry** | Win a ranked match against a Queen. | Win a ranked match against a King. |
| **Royal Couple** | Win a ranked duos match with a Queen as your partner. | Win a ranked duos match with a King as your partner. |
| **Royal Wedding** | Win 10 ranked duos matches with the same Queen. | Win 10 ranked duos matches with the same King. |
| **Battle of the Crowns** | Win a ranked duos match, you and a Queen against two Kings. | Win a ranked duos match, you and a King against two Queens. |
| **Court of Rivals** | Beat 10 different Queens in ranked. | Beat 10 different Kings in ranked. |
| **Usurper** | Beat a Queen ranked two or more tiers above you. | Beat a King ranked two or more tiers above you. |
| **Brotherhood / Sisterhood** | Win a ranked duos match with another King. | Win a ranked duos match with another Queen. |
| **Ruler of the Realm** | Be the top-rated King in your Kingdom for a month. | Be the top-rated Queen in your Kingdom for a month. |
| **Mixed Court** | Win a 1v1v1 against a King and a Queen. | Win a 1v1v1 against a Queen and a King. |

If players also pick a **suit** when they pick King or Queen (Spades, Hearts, Diamonds or Clubs), a few more open up:

| Idea | If you're a King | If you're a Queen |
|---|---|---|
| **Full Deck** | Beat a Queen of every suit. | Beat a King of every suit. |
| **Matching Suits** | Win ranked duos with the Queen of your own suit. | Win ranked duos with the King of your own suit. |
| **Suit Wars** | Beat 5 Kings of the other color (red vs. black). | Beat 5 Queens of the other color (red vs. black). |

Things to decide before these go in:
- **King and Queen leaderboards:** with a King/Queen filter on the Leaderboards, "Ruler of the Realm" becomes easy to track.
- **Suits:** should players also pick a suit? It adds a lot of fun challenges and gives every player a "house".
